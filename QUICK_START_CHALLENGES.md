# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Trainer Challenges

## Get Started in 3 Minutes!

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Challenges (30 seconds)

1. **Login** as a trainer
2. Go to **Trainer Dashboard**
3. Click the **"Challenges"** tab in the navigation
   - Look for the trophy icon 🏆

---

## Step 2: Create Your First Challenge (1 minute)

1. **Click** the orange **"Create Challenge"** button

2. **Fill in the form:**
   ```
   Challenge Name: 30-Day Fitness Challenge
   Description: Complete 30 workouts in 30 days to build consistency
   Goal Type: Workouts
   Target Value: 30
   Duration: 30
   ```

3. **Click** "Create Challenge"

4. ✅ **Done!** Your challenge is now live!

---

## Step 3: Monitor Your Challenge (1 minute)

### View Challenge Card:
- See participants count
- Check days remaining
- View challenge details

### View Leaderboard:
- Click **"View Leaderboard"** button
- See all participants ranked
- Track progress in real-time

### Manage Challenge:
- **Edit:** Click the pencil icon (coming soon)
- **Delete:** Click the trash icon

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

### Creating Effective Challenges:

✅ **DO:**
- Use clear, descriptive names
- Set realistic goals
- Specify exact requirements
- Keep duration reasonable (7-30 days)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make goals too easy or too hard
- Use vague descriptions
- Set extremely short/long durations

## 📊 Dashboard Stats

- **Total Participants** - Sum of all participants

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: How do users join my challenge?
**A:** They go to Community → Challenges and click "Join Challenge"

### Q: Can I edit a challenge after creating it?
**A:** Full edit coming soon. For now, delete and recreate.

### Q: When do users earn badges?
**A:** Automatically when they complete challenges!

### Q: Can I delete a challenge with participants?
**A:** Yes, but it will remove all participant data.
